# Stand shop

Mode: Operate. Scope: simplify the existing /shop, preserve the Teal & Guava site identity, product photographs and working 360° viewer. No new visual world or new image assets.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A customer can understand the physical stand, QR/NFC difference and exact product subtotal before adding it to the bag. Remove the flat Your live design view.

OWN-WORLD: Existing warm ivory, deep teal, pale olive selected states, Noto Sans Arabic and Manrope. Flat sections with clear numbered form groups; restrained rounded selection controls. Product imagery leads rather than generic marketing cards.

STORY: Choose a shape and connectivity; choose a destination; personalize the print; review quantity and an itemized price. QR is always included. Base 350 EGP; NFC +100 per unit, selected explicitly. Mixed-cart totals and exports match.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact site navigation and product heading, current unit price at the opposite edge, three checkout steps. A large full-product photograph and two preview controls share the desktop workspace with shape and connectivity controls. Mobile stacks the product before the form with readable labels and inputs.

FORM: Code-led refinement of the incumbent product configurator. Signature interaction: switching QR/NFC updates unit price, the 3D artwork and the complete order breakdown; no automatic animation. Seed: incumbent-shop-refinement.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Finish record — 2026-10-21

Disposition: ship, with no material fixes from the independent finish review. The build and ten focused browser tests passed; responsive Arabic/English, AA, mixed-cart pricing, local draft/export and 360° evidence is recorded in `docs/VERIFICATION.md` under “Simplified shop and optional NFC”.

The documenter compared the final `shop.css` refinement block, shared global palette/font rules, catalog/default pricing, JSON v2 export, optional-NFC landing copy and README against this contract and the existing root `PRODUCT.md` and `DESIGN.md`. Warm ivory, teal, guava, language-specific Manrope/Noto Sans Arabic stacks and flat section structure remain the incumbent system. Larger form text, explicit connectivity choices and the single itemized purchase panel are local shop refinements; they do not establish global tokens or a replacement visual world. Root design documentation and the design sidecar are preserved. Existing warnings, historical evidence and any unrelated drift are not canonized as design rules.


### Custom shade extension

The print-style section also accepts native color selection or HEX for background/text. Selected colors stay local, update the existing 3D model, and follow drafts, bag editing and JSON export. This is user-owned print data, not a change to the site's design tokens. Existing brand/system files remain unchanged.
